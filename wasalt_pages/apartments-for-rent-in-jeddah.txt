2. Al Murabba

The district is an ancient city in Riyadh with a ton of history. The neighbourhood is a huge tourist attraction for those interested in the upbringing of Riyadh. For instance, there’s the famous Masmak Fortress, where people visit to admire the dazzling architecture of the 19th century. It is widely said that various armies fought over it. Another tourist attraction in Al Murabba district is the famous Murabba Palace which is aesthetically built from clay and mud. The former king of Saudi Arabia, King Abdel Aziz, built the luxurious palace. The palace was meant to be a substantial shelter that is an excellent defence tactic and is difficult to conquer by enemies. Now, it is a great place to visit for tourists and snap memorable pictures. A dim attraction is Deira Square, the execution area, where all the convict beheadings take place at the end of the week.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
1. What is the average price of apartments for sale in Riyadh?
The average price of apartments for sale in Riyadh varies by location and amenities, typically ranging from SAR 300,000 to SAR 1,000,000. Premium areas like Al Olaya and Al Nakheel are priced higher.

2. Which areas in Riyadh offer the best apartments for families?
Popular family-friendly neighbourhoods include Al Malqa, Al Narjis, Al Yasmin, and Al Sahafa — offering good schools, parks, and modern residential buildings.

3. Can foreigners buy apartments in Riyadh?
Yes, non-Saudis can purchase property in Riyadh with government approval. It's recommended to consult with a legal advisor or certified real estate agent for the process and requirements.

4. Are there new apartments for sale with installment plans in Riyadh?
Yes, many developers and real estate platforms offer new apartments for sale in Riyadh with instalment options or financing through banks.
